研究问题

Aging alters the brain's inflammatory profile, which may influence vulnerability to neuroinflammatory insults and the effectiveness of neuroprotective interventions.

方法

This study investigated whether prophylactic administration of L-theanine (LT) and aerobic exercise could attenuate behavioral and molecular changes induced by subchronic systemic lipopolysaccharide (LPS) exposure in aged rats.

核心结论

Three- and eighteen-month-old male Wistar rats received LT, exercise, or both prior to repeated LPS administration.；Behavioral assessments included working, recognition, and spatial short-term memory, followed by hippocampal analyses of inflammatory cytokines, the pCREB/CREB ratio, and Aβ₁-₄₂ expression.；Both aging and LPS administration in 3-month-old rats impaired working memory, spatial short-term memory, and motor activity compared to young adult controls.；Unlike young adult animals, subchronic LPS exposure did not impair cognitive performance in aged rats. ‖ 结论：In conclusion, aged rats exhibited relative resistance to LPS-induced cognitive and inflammatory effects but remained susceptible to amyloid-related changes.
