大麻使用障碍里的白细胞端粒长度与 hTERT MNS16A 变异

研究问题

This study aimed to investigate the association between the human telomerase reverse transcriptase gene (hTERT)-MNS16A polymorphism and cannabis or synthetic cannabinoid use disorder (CUD/SCUD).

方法

The study included a total of 228 participants, comprised of 147 CUD/SCUD patients and 81 healthy controls.；Analysis of the log-transformed T/S ratio indicated a significantly greater degree of LTL shortening in the patient group compared to the control group (p = 0.001).；Among patients, psychotic symptoms and violent behaviors were notably more prevalent in those with a T/S ratio > 1 compared to those with a T/S ratio < 1 (p = 0.022 and p = 0.011, respectively).

核心结论

Genotyping of the hTERT-MNS16A variant was performed through polymerase chain reaction (PCR), while relative LTL was measured using real-time PCR.；For analysis, the T/S ratio (telomere, T) was calculated in relation to a single-copy gene (S), allowing LTL to be expressed as the relative T/S ratio.；Analysis of the log-transformed T/S ratio indicated a significantly greater degree of LTL shortening in the patient group compared to the control group (p = 0.001). ‖ 结论：In summary, although the hTERT-MNS16A variable number tandem repeat polymorphism does not appear to increase susceptibility to CUD or SCUD, LTL shortening is evident in patients with these disorders.
