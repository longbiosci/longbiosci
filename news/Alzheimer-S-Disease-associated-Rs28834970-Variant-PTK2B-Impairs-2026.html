官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

Background: Genome-wide association studies have identified the PTK2B gene, which encodes Proline-rich tyrosine kinase 2 (PYK2), as a genetic risk factor for Alzheimer's disease (AD). However, its role in AD aetiology remains to be understood, as a complex contribution to amyloid {beta} (A{beta}) toxicity, tauopathy and neuronal dysfunction is suggested by contradictory evidence. Determining how the causal variant among AD-associated PTK2B variants impairs PYK2 function to trigger AD hallmarks could bring clarification. Methods: We combined in silico prediction tools with luciferase reporter assays in neuroblastoma cells to prioritize potential AD-associated PTK2B functional variants. We generated isogenic KOLF2.1J induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) carrying the most relevant functional variant using CRISPR-Cas9 and differentiated them into human neuronal cultures (hNCs). We assessed PYK2 expression via immunoblotting, performed transcriptomic profiling using RNA-sequencing, analyzed neuronal network activity via multielectrode array (MEA) recordings, quantified synaptic density through immunocytochemistry in microfluidic devices, and assessed Tau phosphorylation and amyloid-precursor protein (APP) metabolism. Results: The rs28834970 regulatory variant was identified as the most functionally relevant in our model, with the C risk allele promoting transcription factor C/EBP{beta} binding within a cis-regulatory element. In hNCs, the homozygous rs28834970 CC genotype reduced total PYK2 levels by more than 80%, while increasing the proportion of phosphorylated (activated) PYK2. Transcriptomic analysis revealed 724 downregulated and 113 upregulated gene sets, with altered expression of genes involved in extracellular matrix organization, cytoplasmic translation and neurotransmitter secretion. MEA recordings showed reduced network recruitment and altered burst organization. Immunocytochemistry in microfluidic devices consistently revealed that rs28834970 impacts dendritic development and postsynaptic maturation. The risk genotype significantly elevated Tau phosphorylation at pathological sites (Thr181, Thr217, Thr231, Ser202-Thr205, Ser396-Ser404), and enhanced APP processing, resulting in decreased levels of APP and CTF but increased levels of sAPP, sAPP{beta} and A{beta}. Conclusion: The rs28834970 C risk allele mechanistically links AD susceptibility to downregulation of PTK2B/PYK2 expression and to the increase in Tau pathology and A{beta} levels while compromising synaptic structure and function.
