老年人口腔微生物组靶向含片先导试验

这项研究想回答什么

靶向口腔微生物组的含片能否改善老年人的牙周炎症指标？

方法与数据

老年受试者每日 3 次含服 PTx800 或含糖醇的活性安慰剂含片，主要结局为 MGI、PI、BOP、PPD 与 qPCR 病原载量。

核心结论

以下所有数字均可在官方 abstract 原文（见第 7 节）中逐字核对；上游未给出的定量一律写「未给出」，不估算、不换算。

  • 一句话贡献：6 周双盲随机活性安慰剂对照先导试验（最终 n=20）中，PTx800 组探诊出血降低 100%（出血位点完全消退），活性安慰剂组降低 82%，qPCR 显示 Pg、Tf、Td 等红色复合体病原显著减少。

局限与未核验项

最终样本仅 20 例、6 周、单一城市，且通讯作者来自产品供应方（Daily Dental Care, LLC），存在明显利益冲突。

补全文时优先核：若有后续：需看注册信息、随机化与盲法细节、qPCR 病原载量的绝对变化、以及利益冲突管理说明。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

Gingivitis is highly prevalent among older adults and is associated with systemic diseases. Accessible and effective interventions are needed, particularly for underserved populations facing barriers to conventional dental care, such as older adults. This study evaluated the efficacy of a novel, multi-component lozenge (PTx800) designed to modulate the oral environment through blockade of sugar-to-acid and sugar-to-biofilm production of the oral microbiota. A six-week, double-blind, randomized, active placebo-controlled pilot trial was conducted with older adults (final sample n=20) residing in Cleveland, Ohio. Participants were randomized to receive either PTx800 or an active placebo lozenge containing sugar alcohols, used three times daily. Primary outcomes included Modified Gingival Index (MGI), Plaque Index (PI), Bleeding on Probing (BOP), Periodontal Probing Depth (PPD), and changes in oral pathogen load assessed by quantitative PCR. The PTx800 group demonstrated a 100% reduction in BOP, achieving complete resolution of bleeding sites, compared to an 82% reduction in the active placebo group. PPD was stabilized in the PTx800 group and slightly worsened in the control group. Microbiological analysis revealed a significant reduction in the total bacterial load in the PTx800 group, with pronounced decreases in key red complex periodontal pathogens, including Porphyromonas gingivalis (Pg), Tannerella forsythia (Tf), and Treponema denticola (Td). PTx800 lozenges significantly improved clinical and microbiological measures of gingival health in older adults compared to active placebo. These findings suggest that PTx800 is a promising, non-antibiotic approach for managing oral inflammation and dysbiosis in aging populations, with potential implications for reducing oral pathogens strongly linked to systemic diseases.
