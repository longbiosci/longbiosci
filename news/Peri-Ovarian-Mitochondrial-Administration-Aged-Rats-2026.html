卵巢周围给线粒体：游离还是海藻酸包裹的 MSC 来源线粒体

研究问题

Accordingly, mitochondrial mass, the NRF2-dependent antioxidant response, and the glycolytic shift that accompanies mitochondrial insufficiency provide accessible readouts of whether exogenous mitochondria act on the aged ovary through the intended mechanism.

方法

This study used thirty-five female Wistar Albino rats, aged 8 and 15 months, with approval from the Animal Experiments Local Ethics Committee of Kocaeli University (Approval No: KOÜ HADYEK 6/4-2022).；Body weight at the start of the experiment was 218 ± 12.83 g for young rats and 254 ± 14.77 g for old rats.；Animals were bred and housed in-house at the Kocaeli University Experimental Medicine Research and Application Unit (DETAB), where all experimental procedures were also carried out; as animals were not transported from an external source, no acclimatization period was required.；All animals were experimentally naïve and had not undergone any prior procedures, and only clinically healthy females, free of visible coat or structural abnormalities, were selected for the study.

核心结论

Approximately 35% of the encapsulated protein was released within the first hour, followed by cumulative release values of approximately 61%, 83%, and 95% at 2, 3, and 12 h, respectively.；Protein release reached completion (≈100%) by 24 h, indicating that the alginate capsules provided a gradual, extended release rather than an immediate burst release of mitochondria-associated proteins.；Compared with the young control group, the old control group exhibited significantly lower expression of Amhr2 and Fshr (p = 0.005 and p < 0.001, respectively). ‖ 结论：The present findings indicate that localized delivery of alginate-encapsulated MSC-derived mitochondria may provide an effective cell-free approach for improving surrogate markers of ovarian function during aging.
