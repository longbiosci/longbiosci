研究问题

Dementia is one of the leading causes of disability and dependency in older adults [1].

方法

Search Strategy] The units of analysis included in this review are empirical articles published in Scopus, PubMed, and Web of Science.；Reference lists of all included studies were manually searched to identify additional eligible records, adding one study through this method.；Five levels were considered: (1) randomized clinical trial or systematic review/meta-analysis of several studies; (2) prospective cohort study; (3) retrospective cohort study, case–control study, or systematic review of several studies; (4) case series or cross-sectional studies; (5) single case study or expert opinions.

核心结论

Specifically, European Union countries estimate a 58% rise in dementia cases between 2025 and 2050 [2].；Among dementia types, Alzheimer’s disease is the most common type, accounting for 60% to 70% of cases [1].；However, some evidence shows similar dementia progression rates between individuals with SCD (≈7%) and without SCD (≈6%) [13]. ‖ 结论：Ultimately, understanding early hemodynamic patterns in SCD may help identify individuals at higher risk for cognitive impairment/dementia.

局限

However, testing these hypotheses requires future research to address key methodological points.；Future studies should (i) adopt longitudinal and well-powered designs to track fNIRS trajectories in SCD; (ii) improve SCD operationalization to ensure diagnostic consistency; (iii) examine the impact of symptom severity on brain function; (iv) consistently include healthy control groups to establish a baseline for brain activity; (v) include representative samples and balance group sample sizes; (vi) implement challenging cognitive tasks during fNIRS recording to better detect group differences; and (vii) report hemodynamic responses alongside task performance scores.；Third, limited literature…
