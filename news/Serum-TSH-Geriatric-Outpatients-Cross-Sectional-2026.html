研究问题

Serum thyroid-stimulating hormone (TSH) is central to the evaluation of primary thyroid dysfunction, yet its interpretation in later life is not straightforward.

方法

Participants] The clinical screening log documented 477 outpatient encounters.；Twenty-one repeat encounters were removed, leaving 456 unique patients for eligibility assessment.；Patients were excluded for documented hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, or thyroid cancer; levothyroxine use; previous thyroid surgery or radioactive iodine treatment; medications known to affect thyroid function (including amiodarone, glucocorticoids, lithium, levodopa, phenobarbital, phenytoin, carbamazepine, propranolol, propylthiouracil, tyrosine-kinase inhibitors, interferon, interleukin-2, octreotide, dopamine, or dobutamine); active malignancy; or acute infection or inflammatory disease.；Acute illness, including infection and acute flares of malignant or rheumatological disease, was ruled out based on history, physical examination, and laboratory tests; patients with an erythrocyte sedimentation rate ≥ 30 mm/h, C-reactive protein ≥ 5 mg/L, and/or a leukocyte count ≥ 11,000/µL were excluded.

核心结论

Coefficients were exponentiated and reported as percentage differences with 95% CIs.；Their 95% percentile CIs were obtained by case bootstrapping with 5000 resamples (seed 20260829). ‖ 结论：The findings support confirmation of isolated TSH abnormalities and consideration of within-person change and clinical context in older outpatients; whether sex-specific interpretation improves clinical classification cannot be determined from this cohort.

局限

First, the single-center, retrospective, cross-sectional design cannot separate aging from cohort, survivor, or referral effects, and only 17 participants were aged ≥85 years.；Fourth, 250 of the 456 screened patients were excluded, most often for elevated inflammatory markers or insufficient laboratory data, which may have introduced selection bias; individual-level characteristics of the excluded patients were not retained, so they could not be compared with those of the included cohort.
