研究问题

Frailty is a geriatric syndrome characterized by reduced physiological reserve and increased vulnerability to stressors, distinct from the normal aging process [1].

方法

KNHANES is a nationwide, population-based survey designed to assess the health and nutritional status of the Korean population using a stratified, multistage probability sampling design.；KNHANES received approval from the KDCA Institutional Review Board for each survey year, and written informed consent was obtained from all participants (approval numbers 2018-01-03-4C-A for 2022 and 2022-11-16-R-03 for 2024).；Of 13,262 total participants in the KNHANES 2022 and 2024 surveys, 3617 were aged ≥65 years.

局限

Differences in how protein intake was defined—by source, unit, and adjustment for energy—likely account for much of this heterogeneity.；First, the cross-sectional design precludes inference regarding temporality or causality, and reverse causation is possible because frailty itself may influence appetite, food preparation capacity, and both the amount and composition of dietary intake.；Sixth, participants excluded because of incomplete frailty data were older and had less favorable socioeconomic, health, and nutritional profiles than those with complete data, suggesting potential selection bias.
