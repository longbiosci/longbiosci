研究问题

In the present study, we investigated the skin microbiota of a total of 304 healthy volunteers from two distinct age groups using 16S rRNA sequencing.

方法

In the present study, we investigated the skin microbiota of a total of 304 healthy volunteers from two distinct age groups using 16S rRNA sequencing.；OMVs derived from B. fragilis or EVs from S. epidermidis (10 μg in 100 μL volume of PBS) were administered to the skin surface 2 h prior to the application of bacteria (n = 4 for each group).；The strains used in this study were randomly selected.

核心结论

Statistical analysis is by unpaired two-tailed t test. ∗∗∗∗p < 0.0001.；B. fragilis was grown anaerobically in BHI-S (BHI with 0.5% yeast extract and 0.1% cysteine) for 24 h. ‖ 结论：Our comparative microbiota analyses revealed that Bacteroidota, despite their low relative abundance, exert non-negligible functional influence on skin microbial ecology.

局限

Future work will focus on identifying the specific OMV-associated components released by skin-isolated Bacteroides species that initiate autophagosome formation, through integrated proteomic and lipidomic profiling of purified OMVs.；However, 16S rRNA gene sequencing cannot establish the viability, species identity, stable colonization, or anatomical origin of the detected Bacteroides signal; transient environmental deposition and gut-to-skin transfer of Bacteroides species, therefore, cannot be excluded.；Here, we propose a preliminary model in which the anaerobic bacterium Bacteroides induces autophagy through the release of OMVs to clear Pseudomonadota from the skin.
