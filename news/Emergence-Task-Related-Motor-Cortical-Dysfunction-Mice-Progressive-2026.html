MitoPark 小鼠帕金森进程中的 M1 环路功能障碍

这项研究想回答什么

进行性多巴胺变性过程中，初级运动皮层（M1）的环路功能障碍何时、以何种形式出现？

方法与数据

MitoPark 小鼠进行性多巴胺变性模型，M1 锥体神经元细胞与群体水平的纵向编码分析；摘要未给动物数与统计值。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

The primary motor cortex (M1) exhibits substantial changes in Parkinsons disease (PD). However, the temporal relationship between midbrain dopamine (DA) neurodegeneration, M1 circuit dysfunction, and motor symptoms remains poorly understood. Using the MitoPark mice, we investigated the time course of M1 cellular dysfunction during progressive DA loss. MitoPark mice developed gradually impaired skilled movements at a moderate motor stage of Parkinsonism. The motor deficits were associated with reduced cellular activity, impaired neuronal coactivation, and decreased proportion of responsive M1 pyramidal neurons. Moreover, the corticospinal (CSp), but not the intratelencephalic (IT), neurons showed a selective reduction in cellular activity during reaches. At the population level, M1 pyramidal neurons included heterogeneous functional clusters in relation to skilled movements and their stability in longitudinal encoding of movement degraded substantially in MitoPark mice. Together, these results delineate the emergence of M1 circuit pathophysiology at cellular and neural population levels during progressive Parkinsonism.
