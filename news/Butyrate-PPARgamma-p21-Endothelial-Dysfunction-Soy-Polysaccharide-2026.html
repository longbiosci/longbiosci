研究问题

Aging-induced endothelial dysfunction is a core driver of age-related cardiovascular diseases (CVDs).

方法

In 20-month-old naturally aged mice, 12-week soybean polysaccharide (SP) supplementation restored endothelium-dependent relaxation, reduced aortic reactive oxygen species and VCAM-1 in the aortic endothelium, and attenuated colon senescence, improved gut barrier integrity, and reduced systemic inflammation, as shown by decreased serum LPS, IL-1β, TNF-α, and fecal and serum TMA/TMAO levels.

核心结论

SP enriched butyrate-producing gut bacteria, including Lachnospiraceae_NK4A136_group, and increased gut and serum butyrate, consistent with enhanced microbial short-chain fatty acid biosynthesis capacity predicted by PICRUSt2.；Direct butyrate supplementation reproduced these vascular and colonic benefits.；It activated PPARγ signaling and downregulated p21, ICAM-1, VCAM-1, and p16, whereas PPARα and PPARδ remained unchanged. ‖ 结论：It activated PPARγ signaling and downregulated p21, ICAM-1, VCAM-1, and p16, whereas PPARα and PPARδ remained unchanged.
