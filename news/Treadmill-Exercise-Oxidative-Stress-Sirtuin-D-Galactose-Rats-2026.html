恒速跑台在 D-半乳糖大鼠身上改了什么：氧化应激与 sirtuin 生化谱

研究问题

We hypothesized that exercise would differentially affect these markers by intensity and by model group, and that D-galactose-treated rats would show a distinct pattern of response compared with young rats.

方法

Three-month-old male Wistar Albino rats (initial body weight: 220–350 g) were obtained from the Experimental Animals Laboratory of Van Yüzüncü Yıl University.；Only male rats were used to eliminate potential confounding effects of hormonal fluctuations related to the estrous cycle on the measured outcomes.；The rats were maintained in a standard laboratory environment (temperature: 22 ± 2 °C, relative humidity: 55% ± 5%, and a 12/12 h light/dark cycle).；This study was approved by the Van Yüzüncü Yıl University Local Ethics Committee for Animal Experiments (Protocol No: 2022/11-07, date: 3 November 2022) and supported by the Scientific Research Projects Unit of Mersin University (Project No: 2023-2-TP3-4927).

核心结论

In young rats, a statistically significant difference was observed among groups in terms of malondialdehyde (MDA) and sirtuin 2 (SIRT2) levels (p < 0.05), whereas no significant difference was found for peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma coactivator 1-alpha (PGC-1α), brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF), and irisin levels (p > 0.05).；In young rats, analysis revealed a significant difference among groups in MDA levels [F(3,28) = 4.23, p = 0.013, η2 = 0.312]; specifically, MDA levels in the young moderate-intensity exercise group (103.2 ± 10.2 ng/mL) were significantly higher compared to the young control group (90.3 ± 10.4 ng/mL; p = 0.042) and the young high-intensity exercise group (85.9 ± 8.5 ng/mL; p = 0.011).；For SIRT2, the Kruskal–Wallis test showed a significant overall difference among groups [H(3) = 8.224, p = 0.042, ε2 = 0.265]. ‖ 结论：Overall, treadmill exercise produced statistically demonstrated, model-dependent biochemical changes in young rats (SIRT2, MDA, and a significant MDA model × exercise interaction).
