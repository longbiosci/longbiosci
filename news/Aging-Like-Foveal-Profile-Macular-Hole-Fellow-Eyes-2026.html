研究问题

to analyze if healthy fellow eyes (FE) of patients with idiopathic macular hole (IMH) present an aging-like foveal profile.

方法

Foveal characteristics of healthy FE of patients with unilateral IMH are consistent with an aging-like foveal profile.

核心结论

Ophthalmologic examination was performed, including optical coherence tomography (OCT) and OCT-angiography (OCTA).；FAZ area, superficial capillary plexus (SCP) vessel density (VD) and perfusion density (PD), central foveal thickness (CFT), external limiting membrane (ELM), ellipsoid zone (EZ) thickness, and choroidal thickness (ChT) at 5 locations.；Eyes that showed any abnormal findings by OCT were excluded.；The aging-like foveal profile was defined, in accordance with the literature, when the foveal structures exhibited significantly reduced dimensions compared with age-and sex- matched controls, apart from the foveal avascular zone, which is expected to be larger. ‖ 结论：It is plausible to think that this greater physiological deterioration would exacerbate the effect of tangential vitreofoveal traction, thus increasing the risk of idiopathic macular hole.
