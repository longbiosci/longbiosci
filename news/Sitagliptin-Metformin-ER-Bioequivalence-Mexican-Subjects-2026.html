西格列汀/二甲双胍缓释复方在健康墨西哥受试者的生物等效性

来源：Pharmaceuticals (Basel) · PMID 42943229 · DOI 10.3390/ph19101532** · 来源：IMA 知识库《文献层·文献监控卡片》2026-10-29 期 · 全文级（file_id dGBunAeRiRlm，下载实测 692,872 B） · 全文已获取并通过归属校验

研究问题

Type 2 Diabetes (T2D) is a complex metabolic disorder characterized by peripheral insulin resistance and progressive pancreatic β-cell dysfunction, culminating in sustained hyperglycemia [1,2].

方法

Samples were collected at predetermined time points, filtered through 0.45 μm Nylon Titan® membrane filters, and analyzed without replacement of the dissolution medium.；The amounts of dissolved sitagliptin and metformin was quantified by high-performance liquid chromatography coupled with UV detection (HPLC–UV) using a Phenomenex® Luna SCX column (50 × 4.6 mm, 5 μm).；To characterize the pharmacokinetic behavior of the Sitagliptin/Metformin extended-release tablets (50/1000 mg and 100/1000 mg), we conducted four independent, single-center, single-dose, open-label, randomized, two-period, two-sequence (2 × 2) crossover trials under both fasting and fed conditions.；A total of 46 healthy volunteers were randomized in each study.

核心结论

To formally assess bioequivalence, 90% confidence intervals (CIs) were calculated for the geometric mean ratios (Test/Reference) of the log-transformed parameters (Cmax, AUC0–t, and AUC0-∞).；For sitagliptin, the 90% CIs across the fasting and fed studies ranged from 89.35% to 106.72% for Cmax, from 94.27% to 102.58% for AUC0–t, and from 94.13% to 102.49% for AUC0-∞.；Similarly, for metformin, the corresponding 90% CIs ranged from 94.09% to 116.05% for Cmax, from 94.92% to 112.80% for AUC0–t, and from 94.63% to 112.70% for AUC0-∞. ‖ 结论：In conclusion, the generic sitagliptin/metformin extended-release (ER) fixed-dose combinations (50/1000 mg and 100/1000 mg) successfully demonstrated bioequivalence to the reference innovator products under both fasting and fed conditions in healthy Mexican volunteers.

局限

While the present study robustly establishes pharmacokinetic bioequivalence, several avenues for future research remain available to further characterize these formulations’ clinical profile in real-world settings.；Within this context, future studies should evaluate these FDCs in patients with T2D, particularly those with varying degrees of renal function, to confirm broader clinical applicability.
