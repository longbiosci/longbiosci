研究问题

The condition of having a healthy, functional proteome is known as protein homeostasis, or proteostasis.

方法

Maintaining proteostasis is the province of the proteostasis network, which consists of the cellular components that regulate protein synthesis, folding, localization, and degradation.

核心结论

The proteostasis network is a fundamental functional unit in biology with relevance to many diseases.；Despite its importance, the human proteostasis network has until now lacked a precise definition, limiting our ability to interrogate proteostasis, integrate multiomic datasets, compare results across studies, or model proteostasis dysfunction in disease.；To address this limitation, we have operationally defined the human proteostasis network by providing a comprehensive, annotated, and manually curated list of its components classified into nine Branches based on their function and localization - cytonuclear proteostasis, ER proteostasis, mitochondrial proteostasis, nuclear proteostasis, proteostasis network regulation, translation, extracellular proteostasis, the autophagy-lysosome pathway, and the ubiquitin-proteasome system - which… ‖ 结论：These Branches include chaperones, folding enzymes, systems for trafficking proteins, organelle-specific degradation systems, and the cell's translation machinery.
