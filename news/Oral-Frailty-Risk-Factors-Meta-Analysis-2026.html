口腔衰弱的风险因素：五个域的系统综述与 meta

研究问题

Existing studies suggest that risk factors include advanced age, gender, smoking, and malnutrition.[3,8–10] However, due to variations in research methodologies and study populations, several risk factors remain controversial, and definitive risk factors remain elusive.

方法

Inclusion and exclusion criteria] Study Population: Studies enrolling patients diagnosed with oral frailty; Research Design: cross-sectional, case-control, and cohort studies; Data Reporting: Studies reporting ORs and corresponding 95% Cis; Outcome Measures: Studies identifying the presence or absence of oral frailty; Sample Size: Articles involving over fifty subjects; Language: Publications published in English and Chinese.；Exclusion criteria] Full text was not available; Duplicate publications (only one version was retained); Theses, case reports, conference abstracts, and animal research; Incomplete data.；Variables such as the first author, year of publication, country, study location, sample size, study design, and risk factors (only those reported in two or more studies were included), were extracted from eligible studies.；For studies performing both univariate and multivariate analyses, the results from multivariate logistic regression (ORs and 95% CIs) were recorded.

核心结论

Significant heterogeneity was observed across the studies (P < .00001, I2 = 97%).；Similarly, significant heterogeneity was noted across these studies (P < .00001, I2 = 76%).；Following their exclusion (P < .00001, I2 = 32%), a fixed-effects model revealed that females were more likely to develop OF. ‖ 结论：The findings revealed that these risk factors can be categorized into 5 main domains: demographic characteristics, lifestyle habits, medical interventions, past medical history, and clinical assessment scores.
