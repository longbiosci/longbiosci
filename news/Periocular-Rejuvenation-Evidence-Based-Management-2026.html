眶周年轻化：从美观诉求回到眼科证据

研究问题

Periocular ageing affects the skin, eyelids, ocular adnexa, and underlying facial support.

方法

Preference was given to systematic reviews, randomized controlled trials, prospective cohort studies, consensus statements, and clinically relevant recent publications.；Case reports, retrospective series, and expert opinions were included only when higher-level evidence was unavailable or when discussing uncommon complications or emerging therapeutic approaches.；Evidence was appraised qualitatively according to study design, methodological quality, consistency, objective vs. subjective outcomes, duration of follow-up, periocular specificity, and clinical relevance.；Functional disease, active infection or inflammation, uncontrolled systemic disease, and unrealistic expectations should be addressed before elective intervention.

核心结论

In particular, a novel topical formulation containing niacinamide, arbutin, tranexamic acid, ubiquinone, a DHEA-like component, curcuma extract, and marine exopolysaccharides demonstrated substantial efficacy, achieving an average reduction of nearly 48% in under-eye hyperpigmentation after six weeks of twice-daily application without reported adverse effects.；Studies report over 40% improvement in pigmented dark circles with this modality (65).；Deep phenol–croton oil peels have demonstrated substantial and durable clinical improvement, with most patients achieving greater than 50% reduction in pigmentation and sustained benefits lasting up to several years, while maintaining a low complication rate (55). ‖ 结论：Its ophthalmology-oriented approach emphasizes when aesthetic findings overlap with ocular disease, when functional testing or referral is required, and how the strength and source of evidence should qualify technical recommendations.
