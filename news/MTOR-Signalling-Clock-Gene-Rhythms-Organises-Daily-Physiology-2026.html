mTOR 而非时钟基因节律组织日常生理

这项研究想回答什么

日常生理节律（蛋白质组、代谢、药代）是由昼夜时钟还是由 mTOR 组织？

方法与数据

培养细胞、小鼠肝脏与前脑、斑马鱼，以及 Arabidopsis 与 Neurospora 的跨物种药理抑制实验；摘要未给样本量与统计值。

  • 一句话贡献：抑制 mTOR 可消除细胞与机体层面的多数日常生理变异（蛋白质组、RNA 丰度、底物利用、药物代谢），而时钟蛋白节律仍然存在；前脑中蛋白丰度与磷酸化的日常变异大部分丢失，但小鼠与斑马鱼的自主活动与睡眠-觉醒结构大体保留。摘要未给定量。

编辑研判

概念价值高于证据强度：抑制 mTOR 能消除细胞与机体层面多数日常生理变异，而时钟蛋白节律仍在、自主活动与睡眠-觉醒大体保留——若成立，则「昼夜节律随龄退化」与「mTOR 活性随龄改变」这两条线需要重新排座次。跨物种药理实验（小鼠、斑马鱼、拟南芥、脉孢菌）本身也说明这不是哺乳类特例。保留：依赖药理抑制（特异性/组织暴露问题），摘要无任何效应量。对 mTOR 主线值得跟进。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

Daily rhythms in mammalian physiology are widely attributed to rhythmic transcription of "clock-controlled genes" driven by the circadian transcriptional/translational feedback loop (TTFL). Here we show that daily physiology can be uncoupled from the TTFL: inhibiting the mechanistic target of rapamycin (mTOR) abolishes most daily variation in cellular and organismal physiology, whilst clock protein rhythms persist. In cultured cells and mouse liver, functional rhythms are lost, including the proteome, RNA abundance, substrate utilisation and drug metabolism. In forebrain, most daily variation in protein abundance and phosphorylation is lost, yet locomotor activity and sleep-wake architecture are largely preserved in mice and zebrafish. PERIOD2 binds mTORC1 and limits its activity, providing a route by which clock proteins shape daily mTOR signalling. mTOR inhibition preferentially suppresses output over clock gene rhythms in Arabidopsis and Neurospora. We conclude that mTOR is a conserved conduit through which circadian and environmental timing impose daily organisation on physiology.
