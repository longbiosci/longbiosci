肌醇、二甲双胍与小檗碱：PCOS 六个月的观察性比较

来源：J Clin Med · PMID 42941281 · DOI 10.3390/jcm15207765** · 来源：IMA 知识库《文献层·文献监控卡片》2026-10-29 期 · 全文级（file_id dGBunAeRiRlm，下载实测 692,872 B） · 全文已获取并通过归属校验

数据日期 2026-10-29 · 生成时间 2026-10-29 06:03

研究问题

Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) is the most common endocrine disorder affecting women of reproductive age, with a prevalence ranging from 6% to 20%, depending on the diagnostic criteria applied.

方法

The analysis included women who initiated one of the studied therapeutic regimens and completed an uninterrupted six-month course, with clinical and laboratory data available both at baseline and after six months.；The exclusion criteria were: (1) ongoing treatment with combined oral contraceptives, progestins, or other hormonal therapy that could influence metabolic or androgen parameters; (2) treatment with spironolactone; (3) concurrent fertility treatment; (4) discontinuation or change of the initially prescribed therapeutic regimen during the six-month follow-up; and (5) insufficient baseline or six-month clinical or laboratory data for the planned analyses.；These criteria were applied to minimize the potential influence of concomitant therapies on the metabolic and androgen parameters evaluated during follow-up.；The assignment of treatment was not randomized and was based on standard clinical judgment by the treating physician instead of a predetermined trial procedure.

核心结论

Significant differences between treatment groups were observed for BMI (p < 0.001), fasting plasma glucose (p < 0.001), IRI (p < 0.001), HOMA-IR (p < 0.001), total cholesterol (p = 0.04), HDL-C (p = 0.02), and triglycerides (p = 0.02).；In the metformin cohort, decreases in BMI, FPG, IRI, HOMA-IR, total cholesterol, LDL-C, triglycerides, testosterone, androstenedione, DHEAS, and 17-OH-progesterone, and an elevation in HDL-C, persisted statistically significant post-Holm adjustment (all adjusted p < 0.001).；In the myo-inositol cohort, statistically significant decreases were observed in IRI (adjusted p = 0.002), HOMA-IR (adjusted p = 0.009), total cholesterol (adjusted p = 0.042), LDL-C (adjusted p = 0.011), triglycerides (adjusted p = 0.042), androstenedione (adjusted p < 0.001), and DHEAS (adjusted p < 0.001). ‖ 结论：These findings should not be interpreted as evidence of comparative treatment efficacy or superiority because of the observational design, non-randomized treatment allocation, and potential residual confounding.

局限

Notable overall effects of the treatment groups were detected for DHEAS and 17-OH-progesterone; nevertheless, these general results do not pinpoint specific differences between treatment pairs and should be interpreted with caution due to the non-randomized treatment assignment and possible residual confounding.；Nevertheless, direct comparison of the magnitude of these changes with those observed in the other treatment groups is limited by the non-randomized treatment allocation and baseline differences between groups.；Therefore, the observed within-group changes with combination therapy should be considered hypothesis-generating and require confirmation in prospective controlled studies.
