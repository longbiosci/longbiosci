印度教育年限与认知变化的地区异质性

这项研究想回答什么

在低收入/高异质性环境中，教育年限对认知变化的保护作用是否一致？

方法与数据

LASI-DAD 第 1 波（2017-2019）与第 2 波（2022-2024）的纵向认知数据，覆盖 18 个邦与中央直辖区，校正年龄、性别、家庭财富与城乡居住。

核心结论

  • 一句话贡献：2,510 名两波均有评估的 60 岁及以上印度成人中，教育年限与认知变化的交互在两种模型设定下均显著（变化分 F = 2.97，P < .001；协方差分析 F = 4.11，P < .001），各邦变化分估计从奥里萨邦的 -0.056 SD/年教育到喀拉拉邦的 +0.021 SD。

局限与未核验项

仅两波测量，变化分与 ANCOVA 合并估计符号相反（合并值由 -0.022 SD，95% CI -0.027~-0.016 翻转为 +0.031 SD，0.024~0.038），提示模型设定与测量尺度敏感；观察性设计无法排除选择效应。

对本领域的价值

编辑研判

方法学警示价值高于结论：LASI-DAD 两波 2,510 名 ≥60 岁印度成人显示，教育×时间的交互显著（变化分 F=2.97，P<.001；ANCOVA F=4.11，P<.001），各邦估计从奥里萨邦 −0.056 SD/年·教育到喀拉拉邦 +0.021 SD 分布极宽；更关键的是两种模型设定下合并估计符号相反（变化分 −0.022 SD，95% CI −0.027~−0.016 vs ANCOVA +0.031 SD，0.024~0.038）。这提示认知储备类研究的汇总系数对模型设定与测量尺度高度敏感，做跨国比较时必须报告两种设定。仅两波测量、观察性设计无法排除选择效应。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

Higher educational attainment is associated with steeper measured cognitive decline among older adults in India, but whether this association is uniform across the country is unknown. Using the Harmonized Diagnostic Assessment of Dementia for the Longitudinal Aging Study in India (wave 1, 2017-2019; wave 2, 2022-2024), this study examined whether the association between years of schooling and cognitive change varied across 18 states and union territories among 2,510 adults aged 60 years or older who were assessed at both waves. Models adjusted for age, sex, household wealth, and rural or urban residence. Change-score models related inter-wave change to schooling, and analysis of covariance additionally conditioned on baseline cognition. The schooling-by-geography interaction was significant in both specifications (change score, F = 2.97, P < .001; analysis of covariance, F = 4.11, P < .001). State-specific change-score estimates ranged from -0.056 SD per year of schooling in Odisha to +0.021 SD in Kerala. The pooled association reversed sign with baseline adjustment, from -0.022 SD (95% CI: -0.027, -0.016) to +0.031 SD (95% CI: 0.024, 0.038). A single national coefficient conceals substantial geographic variation in how schooling relates to cognitive aging in India.
