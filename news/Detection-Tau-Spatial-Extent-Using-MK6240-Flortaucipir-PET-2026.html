MK6240 与 flortaucipir 的 tau 空间范围比较

这项研究想回答什么

MK6240 与 flortaucipir 两种 tau 示踪剂的空间范围能否互换？

方法与数据

TSE 定义为超过示踪剂特异异常阈值的区域体素百分比，与 SUVR 并行比较，并用 Deming 回归评估互换性（MTL 斜率：TSE 1.29、SUVR 2.69）。

INTRODUCTION: Tau PET enables in vivo quantification of neurofibrillary tangle spread, yet whether tau spatial extent (TSE) performs comparably across tracers remains unknown. METHODS: We performed a head-to-head comparison of Flortaucipir and MK6240 tau PET in 682 individuals across the AD continuum, quantifying TSE as the percentage of regional voxels exceeding a tracer-specific abnormality threshold and evaluated it as a metric of tracer comparability. RESULTS: MK6240 showed greater TSE than Flortaucipir (CU A{beta}+, medial temporal lobe [MTL]: 42% vs 25%; dementia A{beta}+, late neocortical: 55% vs 42%), reaching 50% detection probability at lower A{beta} burden (Centiloid: MTL, 26 vs 45; late neocortical, 95 vs 118). TSE showed higher associations than SUVR with memory, A{beta} PET, and plasma pTau217, particularly in the neo-late composite. Participants with intermediate tau involvement (TSE 25-50%), most below the SUVR positivity thresholds (MTL positivity 10.2-16.9%), had approximately three-fold higher odds of A{beta} PET and pTau217 positivity than TSE 0-25% (ORs 2.90-3.46). 53.4% of intermediate Flortaucipir cases (TSE 25-50%) shifted to a higher MK6240 category, and 82.5% of moderately high Flortaucipir cases (50-75%) were classified as near-saturated (75-100%) by MK6240. Neither raw TSE nor raw SUVR was directly interchangeable across tracers, with systematic and proportional bias for both metrics (MTL Deming slope: TSE 1.29; SUVR 2.69). DISCUSSION: TSE showed stronger unadjusted associations than SUVR in selected region- and outcome-specific comparisons, supporting its potential as a complementary measure. Like SUVR, raw TSE values were not directly interchangeable between flortaucipir and MK6240, and tracer-specific calibration and validation are required before cross-tracer use.
