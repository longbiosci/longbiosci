DLB、AD 与 PDD 的分子差异：多队列整合只给出少量候选

来源：Int J Mol Sci · PMID 42941947 · DOI 10.3390/ijms27209173** · 来源：IMA 知识库《文献层·文献监控卡片》2026-10-29 期 · 全文级（file_id dGBunAeRiRlm，下载实测 692,872 B） · 全文已获取并通过归属校验

研究问题

Dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by dementia accompanied by cognitive fluctuations, recurrent visual hallucinations, rapid eye movement sleep behavior disorder, and spontaneous parkinsonism [1].

方法

A total of 42 samples were included: 9 neurologically unaffected controls, 9 individuals with AD, 12 with DLB, and 12 with PDD.；No new biological samples were generated or experimentally processed, and no chemicals, reagents, laboratory instruments, commercial cell lines, or newly acquired biological materials were directly used in the present secondary analysi.；Source-level preprocessing included robust multi-array average (RMA) background correction, quantile normalization, log2 transformation, and median-polish summarization.；PCA was performed on the resulting centered sample-by-gene expression matrix, and the first two principal components were examined to assess global sample structure, diagnostic-group overlap, and potential outlying samples.

核心结论

The first principal component accounted for 26.86% of the total expression variance, whereas the second principal component accounted for 7.71%.；SLC19A2 exhibited a log2 fold change of −0.670 (95% CI, −0.912 to −0.427; p = 1.76 × 10−6; FDR = 0.02064), VAMP1 a log2 fold change of −1.299 (95% CI, −1.771 to −0.828; p = 1.83 × 10−6; FDR = 0.02064), and COX5B a log2 fold change of −0.869 (95% CI, −1.191 to −0.547; p = 2.59 × 10−6; FDR = 0.02064).；Among these genes, SLC19A2 additionally showed nominal evidence of lower expression in DLB than in AD (log2FC = −0.341, p = 0.01506), although the association did not remain significant after transcriptome-wide multiple-testing correction (FDR = 0.7674). ‖ 结论：The present study identified a limited set of candidate molecular differences among DLB, AD, and PDD rather than a robust transcriptome-wide disease-discriminatory signature.
