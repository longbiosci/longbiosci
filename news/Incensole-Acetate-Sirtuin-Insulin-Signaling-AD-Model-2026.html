乳香醇乙酸酯调节 sirtuin–胰岛素信号轴：实验性 AD 模型

研究问题

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) is a multifactorial, progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by memory loss, cognitive dysfunction, and a decline in problem-solving and decision-making abilities.

方法

Aflatoxin B1 (AFB1), a well-established toxic and carcinogenic compound, was included as a positive control for the toxicity prediction workflow.；This analysis was performed solely to assess the ability of the docking workflow to identify a known SIRT1 ligand binding region and was not used for direct comparison with IA or the reference activator 4TQ.；A total of 51 snapshots were included in the exported MM-PBSA output for each protein–ligand complex.；Entropic contributions were not reported in the exported output files and were therefore not included in the present analysis.

核心结论

Block-based statistical analysis of total MM-PBSA energies revealed no significant difference between SIRT1–4TQ and SIRT1–IA (p = 0.260).；In contrast, significant differences were observed between SIRT3–BBI and SIRT3–IA and between SIRT6–8L9 and SIRT6–IA (p < 0.001 for both comparisons).；Quantitative analysis showed that 75.3% of the analyzed cells were NeuN positive, and 69.5% were synaptophysin positive. ‖ 结论：In conclusion, the present findings indicate that IA treatment is associated with changes in insulin-signaling-related proteins, sirtuin expression, inflammatory and oxidative-stress markers, and PPARα in an Aβ1–42-induced cellular AD model.

局限

IA showed predicted H-HT and DILI probabilities of 0.729 and 0.766, respectively, indicating that possible hepatic toxicity should be considered during further development.
