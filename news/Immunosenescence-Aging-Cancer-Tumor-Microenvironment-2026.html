免疫衰老重塑肿瘤微环境：老年肿瘤治疗的机制与取向

研究问题

Aging is an irreversible, complex biological process characterized by a progressive, steep decline at the organismal level and in immune competence, a phenomenon known as immunosenescence.

方法

In this review, we address this critical gap by proposing three strict criteria that a true immunosenescence marker must fulfill to reliably reflect this process: (1) a progressive onset during aging; (2) a causal relationship with immunological decline, whereby an increase or worsening of the marker directly drives the progression of immune dysfunction; and (3) a capacity for reversibility or modulation upon targeted therapeutic intervention.；We systematically assess some of the hallmarks of aging, including genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, mitochondrial dysfunction, loss of proteostasis, altered intercellular communication, and chronic inflammation, against these proposed criteria to determine their relevance as genuine, actionable hallmarks of immunosenescence.

核心结论

Beyond their baseline anti-inflammatory efficacy, HDACis possess significant therapeutic value in oncology by preventing chimeric antigen receptor T-cell (CAR-T cell) dysfunction and exhaustion; they achieve this by stimulating the wingless-related integration site (Wnt)/β-catenin pathway through the upregulated expression of the essential transcription factors LEF1A and TCF4.；Administration of β-caryophyllene, a naturally occurring terpene that selectively activates cannabinoid receptor 2, restores autophagic ability and prevents hippocampal microglia from releasing the pro-inflammatory cytokines interleukin-1 (IL-1) and IL-6 in aged mice [47].；This age-related molecular pattern and its prognostic impact have not been observed in glioblastoma or colorectal adenocarcinoma [104]. ‖ 结论：Translating these insights into clinically applicable strategies may enable safer and more effective cancer treatments for the growing population of older adults while preserving their quality of life.
