一剂二甲双胍增加雄性 BTBR 小鼠的社交嗅探：品系与性别差异

研究问题

Autism spectrum disorders (ASDs) are lifelong neurodevelopmental conditions commonly diagnosed in childhood when afflicted with both restricted and repetitive traits, along with social deficits [1, 2].

方法

The subjects were 213 mice; 36 BTBR T + Itpr3tf/J (BTBR, JAX stock #002282) females weighing (mean ± standard deviation (SD)) 28 ± 4 g and 39 males 34 ± 5 g, 36 each C57BL/6J (C57, JAX stock #000664) females weighting 23 ± 4 g and males 32 ± 5g, and for 129S1/SvImJ (129S, JAX stock #002448) 30 females weighing 22 ± 3 g and 36 males weighing 28 ± 4 g.；All mice were bred in-house from founders originating from Jackson Laboratories (Bar Harbor, ME, USA).；The housing room temperature was between 22 °C and 25 °C with a 14:10 light-dark cycle (300 lux, as measured by the Lux Light Meter Pro App for iOS) on at 0700 h.；All cages contained 2-5 same-sex mice of the same strain with up to a one-week age difference, ad libitum access to chow (Teklad #7912, Harlan, Madison, WI) and water (reverse-osmosis and acidified to a pH of 2.5–3.0 using HCl).

核心结论

For BTBR mice, the two-way ANOVA interaction for time spent in chambers was significant (F (2, 69) = 3.3, p = 0.04). ‖ 结论：For the reduction of restrictive-repetitive behavior, only C57 females buried fewer marbles after acute metformin treatment, while BTBR, 129S, and male C57 mice were unaffected.

局限

First, the small sample size of mice used for behavioral testing may explain why several effects approached but did not reach significance.；Future studies should take this into consideration to determine the wide range of behavioral effects metformin could have.
