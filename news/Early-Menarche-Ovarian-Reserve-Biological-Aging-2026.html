研究问题

Age at menarche has declined in many populations, but whether earlier menarche is related to ovarian reserve markers and biological aging in young adulthood remains unclear.

方法

In this cross-sectional study, we compared 82 women with early menarche (<12 years) and 92 women with normal menarche (12-13.5 years), all aged 18-25 years, from Anhui Province, China.

核心结论

Serum anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH), follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), luteinizing hormone and estradiol (E2) were measured; antral follicle count (AFC) and ovarian volume were assessed by transabdominal ultrasound; and biological age was estimated using the Klemera-Doubal method (KDM-BA) and Phenotypic Age (PhenoAge).；After adjustment for potential confounders, early menarche was associated with lower AMH (-15.5%, 95% CI: -26.8% to -2.4%) and AFC (-6.2%, 95% CI: -11.4% to -0.8%), higher FSH (22.1%, 95% CI: 5.5% to 41.3%) and E2 (25.7%, 95% CI: 6.5% to 48.3%), and greater KDM-BA acceleration (0.75 years, 95% CI: 0.04 to 1.46), but not with PhenoAge acceleration.；These associations were modest: menarche group explained only 1.4%-4.1% of the variance in these outcomes.；For commercial re-use, please contact reprints@oup.com for reprints… ‖ 结论：These associations were modest: menarche group explained only 1.4%-4.1% of the variance in these outcomes.
