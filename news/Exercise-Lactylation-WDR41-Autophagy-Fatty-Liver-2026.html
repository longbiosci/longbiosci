运动诱导的 WDR41 乳酸化启动自噬并改善脂肪肝

研究问题

Excess lipid accumulation and a sedentary lifestyle are associated with metabolic dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease (MAFLD), but the causal mechanisms and preventive methods are not fully understood.

方法

Macroautophagy/autophagy is a lysosomal degradation pathway robustly induced by exercise and plays a key role in lipid metabolism.

核心结论

Understanding the mechanisms by which exercise activates autophagy and elicits metabolic benefits in the liver will provide important insights into the pathogenesis of MAFLD.；Here, we discovered negative correlations between expression and lactylation of WDR41 (WD repeat domain 41) and pathogenesis of a series of human hepatic metabolic diseases.；We found that WDR41 was a pro-autophagy protein that underwent lactylation in response to autophagy-inducing conditions, such as exercise.；WDR41 was lactylated at lysine 319 (K319), a modification facilitated by LDHA (lactate dehydrogenase A) upon autophagy induction, and delactylated by SIRT2 (sirtuin 2). ‖ 结论：Thus, we demonstrated that WDR41 lactylation at K319 is an important regulatory mechanism by which exercise regulates autophagy and hepatic glucose and lipid metabolism.
