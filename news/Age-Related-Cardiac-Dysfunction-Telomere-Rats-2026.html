大鼠心功能随龄下降，但端粒长度没变：一个阴性结果

研究问题

Ageing is a well-known, independent risk factor for cardiovascular disease (CVD) (1).

方法

All procedures involving animals were approved by the Norwegian Food Safety Authority under FOTS ID 20208, and were carried out in compliance with national regulations for the care and use of laboratory animals and the ARRIVE 2.0 guidelines (12).；[Animals] Sprague-Dawley rats from ENVIGO were clinically healthy upon inclusion and had not been exposed to any experimental procedures.；Rats that met predefined humane endpoints during the study were excluded (n = 12).；Included animals were euthanized at one of four predefined age points: 3 months [n = 9, mean body weight (BW) 363 g], 9 months (n = 9, mean BW 506 g), 16 months (n = 13, mean BW 634 g), and 24 months (n = 20, mean BW 596 g).

核心结论

The cutoff was set to the mean value + >2 SDs at 9-months, except from LVEF where the threshold was set to 40% to reflect the traditional clinical definition of HF with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF).；Cutoff for stratification was set at the mean + >2 SD of the 9-month group, except for LVEF, where the cutoff was set at 40% to reflect the traditional clinical definition of heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF).；TL, Tibia length; AU, arbitrary units; ns, not significant, * p < 0.05. ‖ 结论：In the preclinical Sprague-Dawley rat model, which displayed a cardiac phenotype resembling human ageing over 2 years, we found no changes in neither leukocyte nor cardiac telomere lengths, suggesting a translational gap vs. humans, where shortening telomere lengths are well-established in ageing.
