研究问题

In adults with diabetes, cardiovascular disease (CVD) remains a major contributor to mortality despite contemporary multifactorial risk control and cardioprotective therapy [1, 2].

方法

Participants were excluded if they were younger than 20 years (n = 46,235) or pregnant at the time of the survey (n = 1,547), leaving 53,534 individuals.；We further excluded participants without linked mortality follow-up (n = 2,732), those without diabetes or prediabetes (n = 38,312), and those without CVD (n = 9,671), yielding 2,819 eligible participants.；Oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT)-based criteria were applied only in NHANES cycles and participants with available OGTT data.；The corresponding NHANES variables were MCQ160B–MCQ160F, and participants with a “Yes” response to any of these items were classified as having CVD.

核心结论

[Statistical analysis] All analyses used R 4.4.1 (R Foundation); two-sided P < 0.05 denoted statistical significance.；Survey-weighted Cox proportional hazards models were used to estimate hazard ratios (HRs) and 95% confidence intervals (CIs) for the associations of ALI, ePWV, and joint ALI–ePWV categories with all-cause, cardiovascular, and premature mortality.；Mean age was 66.56 years (SE 0.33) and 41.07% were women; mean BMI and eGFR were 32.41 kg/m² (SE 0.25) and 71.46 mL/min/1.73 m² (SE 0.71), respectively. ‖ 结论：Because ALI and ePWV are derived from routinely available measurements, their combined assessment may help describe mortality-risk patterns in this population.

局限

First, selection bias and generalizability should be interpreted cautiously.；Third, residual and unmeasured confounding cannot be excluded despite extensive adjustment, which is a common limitation of observational studies [47].；Fifth, the cut-points for ALI and ePWV were selected using maximally selected rank statistics and should be considered exploratory.
