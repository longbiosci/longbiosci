研究问题

Age-related macular degeneration (AMD) is one of the leading causes of irreversible visual impairment and blindness among older adults and represents a major global public health concern.

方法

A global systematic review and meta-analysis published in 2014 estimated that, among individuals aged 45–85 years, the prevalence of early AMD, late AMD, and all AMD was 8.0%, 0.37%, and 8.7%, respectively, corresponding to an estimated global population of approximately 196 million individuals with AMD in 2020 [1].；As a narrative review, this article was based on a targeted literature search and critical selection of relevant publications rather than a systematic review methodology.；In this review, diesel exhaust is not considered an independently established risk factor for AMD but rather as a complex oxidative stress-inducing exposure within the broader spectrum of traffic-related air pollution.；A systematic review examining multiple air pollutants, including PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3, and SO2, identified relatively few studies evaluating SO2 in relation to AMD, precluding firm conclusions regarding the strength or consistency of this association [24].

核心结论

A global systematic review and meta-analysis published in 2014 estimated that, among individuals aged 45–85 years, the prevalence of early AMD, late AMD, and all AMD was 8.0%, 0.37%, and 8.7%, respectively, corresponding to an estimated global population of approximately 196 million individuals with AMD in 2020 [1].；In a longitudinal study of 39,819 individuals in Taiwan, long-term exposure to NO2 and CO was associated with an increased risk of incident AMD, with individuals in the highest exposure categories showing approximately 1.9-fold and 1.8-fold higher risks, respectively, than those in the lowest exposure categories [19].；These findings highlight the importance of evaluating environmental chemical exposures not only individually but also as co-exposures, because simultaneous exposure to multiple toxicants may produce additive, synergistic, or otherwise interactive biological… ‖ 结论：Moving toward exposure-informed, mechanism-based, and biomarker-guided prevention is a reasonable direction for future AMD research, but its clinical value remains to be demonstrated.
