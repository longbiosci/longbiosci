研究问题

Still, exactly how DHM connects antioxidant activity to mitochondrial quality control and metabolic remodeling in a living, aging organism remains poorly defined.

方法

Working solutions of 50, 100, and 200 μM DHM were prepared fresh in 0.5% (v/v) DMSO vehicle for every experimental condition.；The three concentrations tested fall within the range typically used to assess flavonoid bioactivity in this organism [21,22,23].；Lifespan Assay] Survival was measured in liquid culture following a protocol adapted from our prior work [23].；Synchronized L4-stage larvae (approximately 10 animals per well) were dispensed into 96-well plates containing S-complete medium, E. coli HB101 at the working concentration, 50 μM FUdR to block progeny production, and either DHM (50, 100, or 200 μM in 0.5% DMSO) or vehicle alone.

核心结论

DHM increased mean lifespan to 21.78 ± 0.32, 22.45 ± 0.33, and 22.96 ± 0.34 days at 50, 100, and 200 μM, respectively, corresponding to extensions of approximately 9.1%, 12.5%, and 15.0%.；Log-rank analysis confirmed significant survival benefits at all three concentrations (50 μM, 100 μM, and 200 μM, each p < 0.0001). ‖ 结论：Overall, redox-related, mitochondrial, and lipid-related readouts changed alongside the observed healthspan-related phenotypes; their causal relationships remain unresolved.
