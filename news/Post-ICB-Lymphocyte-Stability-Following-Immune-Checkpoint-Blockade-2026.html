淋巴细胞稳定性与线粒体单倍型预测 ICB 结局

这项研究想回答什么

线粒体单倍型与免疫检查点治疗后的淋巴细胞稳定性是否共同决定 ICB 结局？

对本领域的价值

线粒体遗传变异 × 免疫老化的交叉，属长尾命中，但概念可迁移至老年免疫与免疫衰老，值得跟进补看定量。

编辑研判

概念可迁移是它唯一的卖点：线粒体遗传变异（HG-T 单倍型，约 10% 白人英国人）与 ICB 后淋巴细胞计数不稳（LS-U，post-ICB/基线 ≤0.8）及较差总生存相关，并经 LS 中介——「线粒体基因组背景 → 免疫细胞稳态 → 临床结局」这条链条对老年免疫衰老有参照意义。硬伤：摘要未给总样本量、HR 与 CI，单倍型分析限白人英国人群。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

Early biomarkers of durable response to immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) are urgently needed. We identify lymphocyte stability (LS) - preservation of peripheral lymphocyte counts after one cycle of ICB - as an on-treatment predictor of overall survival (OS) in metastatic melanoma (MM): patients with unstable lymphocyte counts (LS-U; post-ICB/baseline [&le;]0.8) have markedly increased risk of death. Metabolic profiling of patient T cells demonstrates ICB enhancement of mitochondrial bioenergetics, whilst CD8+ T cells from LS-U patients mount an exaggerated transcriptional response to ICB incorporating upregulation of mitochondrial pathways. Given these findings we assessed the relationship of LS with mitochondrial haplogroup, revealing carriage of haplogroup T (HG-T), present in ~10% of white British individuals, is associated with both LS-U and impaired OS, with mediation analysis implicating LS mediation. HG-T carriers have higher CD8+ T-cell pre-treatment mitochondrially encoded transcript abundance and, in paired scRNA-seq data, fewer progenitor-exhausted cells, ICB hyperresponsivity, apoptotic program induction and reduced clonal persistence, consistent with baseline mitochondrial priming. These findings position LS as a clinically accessible marker of ICB outcome linked to inherited mitochondrial variation.
