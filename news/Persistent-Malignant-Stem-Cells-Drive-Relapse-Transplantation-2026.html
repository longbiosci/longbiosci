残留恶性 HSC 驱动 MDS 移植后复发

这项研究想回答什么

移植后残留的恶性 HSC 能否用超低起始量测序提前预测 MDS 复发？

方法与数据

多中心移植后队列 + 超低投入测序，并以单细胞蛋白/DNA/RNA 与线粒体谱系追踪定位 MDS 细胞；摘要未给队列总例数。

官方摘要原文（逐字，供回溯）

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

The myelodysplastic neoplasms (MDS) are initiated in hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs). Allogeneic transplantation is the only curative treatment, but relapse remains common. To detect rare MDS HSCs, we developed an ultra-low-input DNA sequencing assay and applied it to a multicenter post-transplant cohort. HSC mutation detection preceded relapse by a mean of 98 days and strongly predicted relapse (95.7% vs. 30.3%; hazard ratio, 8.8; P=1.5e-7). Mutations detected only in unfractionated marrow did not predict relapse. We developed methods to measure donor-host HSC chimerism, improving relapse prediction sensitivity. Single-cell protein, DNA, and RNA analyses with mitochondrial lineage tracing confirmed MDS cell enrichment within immunophenotypically- and transcriptionally-defined HSCs, identifying shared ribosomal gene downregulation and candidate mechanisms of venetoclax sensitivity. Venetoclax/azacytidine depleted detectable MDS HSCs in longitudinally evaluated patients. These findings establish that HSC mutation detection predicts post-transplant relapse, provide a rationale for preemptive intervention, and define shared biological features of transplant-resistant MDS HSCs.
