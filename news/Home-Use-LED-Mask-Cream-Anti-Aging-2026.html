核心结论

Compared to untreated cultures, elastin expression was 26.1% higher in the cultures treated with the LEDs alone and 29.6% higher in the cultures treated with the LEDs and the cream, and collagen 1 expression was 7.6% higher in the cultures treated with the LEDs and the cream (p< 0.05 for all comparisons).；Significant differences in elastin and collagen 1expression were also observed between the cultures treated with the LEDs and the cream versus the cultures treated with the cream alone (p = 0.031 for elastin and p = 0.033 for collagen 1).；Most women (n = 21; 42%) had a mixed (dry, normal, and/or oily) skin. ‖ 结论：Our study confirmed the anti‐aging effects of red and infrared LED treatment previously reported in the literature, and provided a thorough assessment of the efficacy of the study products.
