研究问题

While studies have examined brain age in GHR [15, 16], it remains unclear whether such individuals exhibit brain age differences during adolescence and early adulthood brain development.

方法

This study enrolled 240 participants aged 12–25 years, including 83 SZ patients, 48 individuals with GHR, and 109 HC.；Patients diagnosed with SZ were recruited from two clinical facilities: the First Affiliated Hospital of China Medical University and the Shenyang Mental Health Centre.；The GHR group was comprised solely of first-degree relatives of SZ patients, enrolled from the same institutions, while HC group was drawn from the local community through targeted advertising efforts.；Participants in the SZ group met the DSM-IV-TR diagnostic standards, whereas individuals in GHR group had no recorded history of any Axis I disorders, and HC reported no such disorders either personally or within their families.

核心结论

ANOVA revealed significant differences among these groups (F [2,237] = 31.91, P < .001, eta² = 0.21, 95% CI [0.124, 0.295]; Fig.；Specifically, SZ group showed notably greater BrainAGEcorr values than HC (+ 3.66 years, P < .001) group and GHR (+ 2.39 years, P < .001) group, while GHR group also showed significantly greater values than HC group (+ 1.27 years, P = .021).；The box plot illustrates the mean values along with their corresponding standard deviations. *P < .05, *P < .001 [Brain age gap differences in groups acco] When controlling for age, sex, education years, and BMI in an ANCOVA, significant group differences in BrainAGEcorr values among three groups were also observed (F [2,233] = 18.86, P < .001, eta² = 0.14). ‖ 结论**：A greater brain age gap could be associated with the onset and progression of executive deficits in SZ.

局限

Notably, cross-sectional design restricts the ability to determine causal links between brain age acceleration and executive dysfunction among GHR.；Thirdly, future research should further construct and apply interpretable brain age models (i.e., regional brain age models) to reveal the spatial specificity and neural mechanisms behind accelerated brain aging.
