核心结论

Histological and in vivo imaging studies have consistently demonstrated that retinal ganglion cells (RGCs) decline at an annual rate of approximately 0.5%–0.6%, corresponding to a loss of 6,000–6,700 RGCs per year (Vilasboas-Campos et al., 2025).；Specifically, each additional year of age acceleration measured using the Horvath clock was associated with 15% higher odds of fast progression (OR, 1.15; 95% CI, 1.07–1.23; P < 0.001).；The prevalence of DR is closely tied to the worldwide epidemic of diabetes, with recent meta-analyses estimating a global prevalence of any DR at approximately 35%, including proliferative forms and clinically significant macular edema. ‖ 结论：These four were selected from a heterogeneous landscape of over 40 identified supplements because they uniquely converge on aging-related biological pathways across all three diseases examined, maintain a coherent translational trajectory from bench to early clinical evidence, and raise clinically actionable questions around dosing, safety, and patient counseling.

局限

The heterogeneity of the included interventions and evidence—including preclinical studies, observational studies, and clinical trials—limits direct comparisons and precludes conclusions regarding comparative efficacy.；These limitations should be considered when interpreting the proposed framework and its potential clinical implications.
