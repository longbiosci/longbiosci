GLP-1 受体激动剂与认知：AD 高危人群 RCT 的 meta 分析

来源：Nutrients · PMID 42942883 · DOI 10.3390/nu18203312** · 来源：IMA 知识库《文献层·文献监控卡片》2026-10-29 期 · 全文级（file_id dGBunAeRiRlm，下载实测 692,872 B） · 全文已获取并通过归属校验

数据日期 2026-10-29 · 生成时间 2026-10-29 06:03

研究问题

Alzheimer’s disease (AD) currently burdens over 55 million people on a worldwide scale and is predicted to triple by 2050 [1].

方法

Eligible GLP-1RA agents included: lixisenatide, liraglutide, semaglutide, albiglutide, dulaglutide, exenatide in the search.；We included studies evaluating the use of GLP-1RA(s) independently of nutritional status, on cognitive performance evaluation tests following treatment.；Studies providing raw mean and standard deviation of cognitive tests for the outcome of interest were also included in the meta-analysis.；Exclusion criteria included: (i) non-original research (including reviews, comments, editorials, perspectives), (ii) non-English language because the review team did not have sufficient linguistic resources to reliably assess eligibility, methods and data extraction, (iii) retracted publications, (iv) animal or in vitro studies, (v) conference abstracts, (vi) lack of specific information for performing a meta-analysis.

核心结论

The pooled random-effects estimate showed no evidence of a treatment effect (Hedges’ g = 0.014, 95% CI: −0.061 to 0.088; p = 0.515), with tau2 = 0 and I2 = 0%.；In the sensitivity analysis excluding the dominant Cukierman trial [24] from global cognition, the remaining two studies yielded g = −0.205 (95% CI: −2.678 to 2.269; p = 0.484), with tau2 = 0 and I2 = 0%. ‖ 结论：Larger randomized controlled trials specifically designed to assess cognition, with standardized and prespecified cognitive outcomes and adequate follow-up, are required to determine whether GLP-1RA(s) hold potential in adults at risk of developing AD.

局限

Given heterogeneity in study populations, pharmacological formulations, dosing regimens, and intervention periods, current evidence remains insufficient to draw substantiated conclusions about the efficacy of GLP-1RA(s) in patients with MCI.；With only two or three studies in pooled analyses, estimates of between-study heterogeneity are imprecise; therefore, tau2 and I2 should be interpreted cautiously.；An additional limitation is that no single cognitive domain, instrument, or assessment time point was prespecified as the primary cognitive outcome in the registered protocol.
