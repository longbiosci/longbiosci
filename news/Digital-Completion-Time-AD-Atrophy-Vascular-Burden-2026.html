数字连线测试时间：更贴近 AD 型萎缩，而非血管负荷

研究问题

15 The two processes differ in their structural signatures yet both degrade processing efficiency, and whether digital slowing preferentially reflects one over the other has not been directly examined.

方法

Written informed consent was obtained from all participants before enrollment.；[Missing data] Five participants without MMSE scores were excluded.；[Convergent validity of WML volume] To assess the convergent validity of BAAD‐derived WML (log WML/TIV), we examined its association with visual PVH and DSWMH ratings (0–4, Shinohara classification, 21 Japanese Brain Dock Guidelines 2019, 22 ); because volumetric quantification uses T1‐weighted images and visual ratings use independent FLAIR images, agreement reflects cross‐sequence convergent validity.；Time‐ratio features (each item's time divided by the total) were included only in the tree‐based models (XGBoost, residual stacking), since these compositional features induce multicollinearity in linear models.

核心结论

[Convergent validity of WML volume] BAAD‐derived WML volume (from T1) agreed with the visual ratings from the independent FLAIR images: log WML/TIV correlated strongly with PVH grade (Spearman ρ = 0.752; 95% CI, 0.722–0.779) and DSWMH grade (ρ = 0.720; 95% CI, 0.690–0.749), increasing monotonically across grades (Jonckheere–Terpstra and Kruskal–Wallis, P < 0.001); the most severe grades were infrequent, so some post hoc comparisons did not reach significance.；XGBoost gave the largest ΔR (+0.054; 95% CI, +0.010 to +0.095; p = 0.013) and a significant reduction in MAE (p = 0.003). ‖ 结论：We interpreted these findings as a difference in degree rather than a dissociation.

局限

The main limitation is the absence of AD‐specific biomarkers: neither amyloid or tau imaging nor validated blood‐based markers were available.；The magnitude of the residual‐correlation difference should therefore be interpreted with caution.
