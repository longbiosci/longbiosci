研究问题

Intracerebral hemorrhage (ICH) is a life-threatening neurological disease, with a 30-day mortality of approximately 40%1, 2.

方法

The 18-month-old C57BL/6 male mice were provided by the National Institute on Aging (NIA).；Mice were treated with either CCL11-neutralizing antibody (clone 42285, MAB420; R&D Systems, Minneapolis, MN, USA) or IgG2A isotype control (clone 54447, MAB006; R&D Systems) at 0.1 mg/kg via intraperitoneal injections (i.p.) every other day, for a total of 10 injections, prior to ICH surgery.；All animal experiment procedures followed the National Institutes of Health guidelines for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals and were approved by the Animal Welfare Committee of the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston.；Briefly, the animals were anesthetized with isoflurane (4%, induction; 1.5%, maintenance) and placed in a stereotactic frame.

核心结论

结论：Our present findings support previous studies showing that aging shifts MG toward being less phagocytic and producing more pro-inflammatory cytokines and ROS39, 40.

局限

Therefore, testing neutralization of circulating CCL11 in aged subjects as a therapeutic target for post-ICH recovery warrants further investigation.
