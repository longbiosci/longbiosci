PINK1/Parkin 介导的线粒体自噬在壬基酚致足细胞损伤中的保护作用

研究问题

Podocyte injury is an early and critical event in the development of glomerulonephritis (GN).

方法

Nonylphenol (NP) can accumulate in the kidney and induce nephrotoxicity.

核心结论

However, the mechanism by which NP promotes GN progression by inducing podocyte apoptosis remains unclear.；This study aimed to determine whether NP exposure activated PTEN-induced kinase 1/Parkin RBR E3 ubiquitin protein ligase (PINK1/Parkin)-mediated mitophagy, leading to mitochondrial homeostasis imbalance in podocytes and promoting apoptosis, thereby inducing pathological changes associated with GN in mice.；Cell experiments were performed in Mouse Podocyte Clone-5 (MPC-5) cells by activating or inhibiting Parkin.；In vivo, 72 male wild-type and Parkin-/- C57BL/6 mice were treated with different doses of NP by gavage for 90 days to investigate the role of Parkin in NP-induced renal injury. ‖ 结论：Collectively, Parkin-mediated mitophagy plays a key protective role in maintaining podocyte homeostasis and alleviating glomerular structural and functional damage.
