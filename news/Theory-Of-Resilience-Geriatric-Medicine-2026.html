研究问题

This review examines current perspectives on the theory of resilience as a promising concept in geriatrics and longevity medicine.

方法

Modern data on the biological mechanisms of aging, risk factors for age-associated diseases, the concept of biological age, epigenetic clocks, and proxy diseases of aging were analyzed.

核心结论

It has been demonstrated that chronological age does not adequately reflect individual differences in aging rates and functional status.；Therefore, particular importance is attributed to integrated indicators capable of assessing adaptive reserves and the organism's resistance to age-related changes.；The review discusses the potential application of epigenetic clocks as molecular biomarkers of biological aging, as well as the role of sarcopenia, frailty, cognitive impairment, and metabolic disorders as proxy diseases of aging.；These conditions are shown to reflect the systemic nature of age-related changes and to be associated with decreased functional independence and increased mortality risk. ‖ 结论：These conditions are shown to reflect the systemic nature of age-related changes and to be associated with decreased functional independence and increased mortality risk.
