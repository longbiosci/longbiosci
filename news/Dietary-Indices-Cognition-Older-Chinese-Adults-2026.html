cMIND、膳食多样性与 HPDI：中国老年人认知的三个饮食指数

研究问题

The key question is therefore not only whether diet is associated with cognition, but which dietary dimensions are being captured and whether they relate to cognitive health in similar ways.

方法

Follow-up surveys have subsequently been conducted in 2000, 2002, 2005, 2008 to 2009, 2011 to 2012, 2014, 2017 to 2018, and 2021.；Participants were eligible for the present analysis if they were aged 65 years or older, had complete information required to derive the three dietary indices, had valid cognitive outcome data, and had a valid positive 2021 sampling weight.；Of the 9720 participants in the 2021 CLHLS, 101 were excluded because of incomplete dietary information, and a further 4 were excluded because of missing or invalid sampling weights, leaving 9615 participants in the final analytic sample.；Participants with missing covariate data were retained because these values were subsequently handled using multiple imputation.

核心结论

The mean age was 84.62 years (SD, 10.80), with 21.0% of participants aged 65 to 74 years, 31.2% aged 75 to 84 years, 36.0% aged 85 to 99 years, and 11.8% aged 100 years or older.；Women accounted for 53.7% of the analytic sample, and 69.4% of participants lived in urban areas.；Overall, 42.2% were married and living with a spouse, 38.9% had two or more chronic diseases, and 28.5% had ADL disability. ‖ 结论：In conclusion, cMIND, dietary diversity, and HPDI were all positively associated with global cognitive function in older Chinese adults, with associations also evident for specific cognitive domains.

局限

Age-related heterogeneity was another notable finding of this study.；The contrasting findings across studies suggest that age-related heterogeneity may depend on the dietary exposure, cognitive outcome, study design, and age distribution of the population examined.；Taken together, these considerations suggest that age-related heterogeneity warrants explicit evaluation in diet–cognition research, particularly in cohorts spanning a wide range of later life.
