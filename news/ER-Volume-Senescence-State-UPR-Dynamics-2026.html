核心结论

When comparing the expression levels of individual key genes, we observed 6-fold higher expression of SEC61A1, a core translocon component; a 56-fold increase in CKAP4 (CLIMP-63), which anchors the ER to microtubules; and an 11-fold elevation in HSPA5 (BiP), the primary ER chaperone.；Specifically, we noted divergent expression of reticulons, which stabilize ER membrane curvature: RTN2 expression was upregulated 39-fold, which may reflect active secretory traffic, while RTN4 showed a 2.4-fold increase, suggesting a moderate expansion of the tubular ER in EA.hy926 cells.；In EA.hy926 cells, ATF6 expression was 1.6-fold higher, concurrently with its target genes SEL1L (an ERAD-associated protein component) and HERPUD1, which showed 1.3-fold and 2-fold increases, respectively. ‖ 结论：We demonstrate that UPR dynamics are significantly modulated by cellular geometry.
