研究问题

Life expectancy among individuals with Profound Intellectual and Multiple Disabilities/Polyhandicap (PIMD/PLH) has increased, making information essential for age-appropriate care.

方法

This cross-sectional study used data from the French PIMD/PLH cohort.

核心结论

Inclusion criteria were (i) age > 3 years; (ii) PIMD/PLH diagnosis.；Data on health characteristics and health care consumption were included.；Adults were compared by age (≤ 45 vs. > 45 years). ‖ 结论：This study highlights the need for lifelong medical and rehabilitative care in PIMD/PLH.
