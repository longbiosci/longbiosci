这项研究想回答什么

MEG 的组水平差异能否转译为个体水平的疾病分型？

方法与数据

临床表型 + 功率谱密度 + 动态因果模型推断的潜网络机制，以分类作为探索性探针评估组水平到个体水平的转译。

局限与未核验项

样本量中等（对照 74、bvFTD 45、PSP 55），分类仅为探索性探针且摘要未给置信区间，两种疾病临床与生理特征部分重叠。

展开 api.biorxiv.org / api.medrxiv.org 返回的 abstract 原文

Precision medicine approaches require physiological effects to be informative when translated to the individual. Reliable group differences may have limited individual value when distributions overlap, mechanisms are heterogeneous, or patients differ in phenotype. A feature can differ between group means and still overlap heavily across individuals Moreover, combinations of physiological abnormalities may reveal subject-level structure that is not captured by any single marker at the group level. We evaluate the translation between group- and individual-level features derived from resting-state magnetoencephalography (MEG) using behavioural-variant frontotemporal dementia (bvFTD) and progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP) as demonstrator conditions. These illnesses have distinct molecular pathology but share the pressing need for therapeutic development and have partially overlapping clinical and physiological features. We combine clinical phenotype, power spectral density (PSD), and latent network mechanisms inferred by dynamic causal modelling (DCM) to compare groups and to access their individuals mapping translations. We used classification as an exploratory probe of how group-defined physiological features from 74 healthy controls, 45 participants with bvFTD and 55 participants with PSP support individual assignment. Clinical scores showed strong individual-level translation, consistent with clinical diagnostic definitions (e.g, controls-vs-PSP accuracy 0.94; bvFTD-vs-PSP accuracy 0.83). MEG spectra feature classified individuals weakly (accuracy ~0.6), despite group level re-organization (e.g., reduced beta-band and increased fronto-insular alpha-phase/beta-amplitude coupling in bvFTD). Inferred biophysical parameters gave intermediate performance (e.g. PSP-vs-control accuracy 0.70; bvFTD-vs-PSP accuracy 0.74) while retaining mechanistic interpretability. Using this approach, experimental medicine studies of neurological disease can formally trade between mechanistic insight and classification performance, in using biomarkers that can account for individual heterogeneity within and between diagnostic groups.
